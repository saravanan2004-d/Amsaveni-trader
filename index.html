Who We Are

About Amsaveni Traders

AMSAVENI TRADERS is a trusted commission shop dealing with various agricultural products, pulses, millets, seeds, and grains. We provide quality products and reliable trading services for farmers and merchants.

Based opposite Lysander Hospital in Virudhunagar, Tamil Nadu, we sit at the crossroads of the region's growers and its wider markets — a commission house where farmers bring their harvest and merchants find consistent, well-graded supply. Every transaction is handled with transparent weighment and fair commission.
